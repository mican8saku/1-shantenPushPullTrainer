How to Play

About

Overview

A training tool for the push/fold decision in riichi mahjong. Every question shows a 1-shanten hand facing a riichi and walks you through the same three steps: count the passed sujis, estimate the deal-in rate, then compare it against your hand's threshold.

Credits

Designed and built by kakino_tea. Single-page HTML/CSS/JS app with no backend and no tracking — high scores and custom-practice settings live in your browser's localStorage.

Sources

Threshold tables and deal-in rate estimates are adapted from commonly circulated 押し引き (push/fold) reference charts for riichi mahjong. Rates are training estimates and are not a substitute for table feel. Tile images are loaded from ./tiles/; if an image is missing, a text fallback is drawn instead.
