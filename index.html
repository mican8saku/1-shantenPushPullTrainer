How to Play

About

Overview

A training tool for the push/fold decision in riichi mahjong. Every question shows a 1-shanten hand facing a riichi and walks you through the same three steps: count the passed sujis, estimate the deal-in rate, then compare it against your hand's deal-in threshold values.

Sources

Shape tiers and Deal-in Threshold tables are adapted from Unimaru(うに丸)'s book covering this topic. Deal-in rate estimates are adapted from Statistical Mahjong Strategy by Miinin. The deal-in rates are rough training estimates and are not exact as they fall into a bucket of 5% so there is bound to be inaccurate especially if you factor in other factors in there too or apply it to a specific concrete discard pond.
